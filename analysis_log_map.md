# Joining Gallup and IHME for the map

- Gallup: 144 countries matched to ISO codes (22 by hand, e.g. 'Turkiye', 'Congo (Kinshasa)').
- Joined by ISO code: 207 places in total; 141 have both datasets, 63 only IHME, 3 only Gallup (Kosovo, Northern Cyprus and Hong Kong are not separate places in IHME).
- Claim test (141 countries with both datasets): Spearman correlation between the share of women who saw a health professional and estimated PCOS prevalence = 0.25 (p = 0.0026). Moderate, statistically significant.
- Median PCOS in the quarter of countries where women see professionals most: 6.12% (1 in 16); least: 1.48% (1 in 68).
- Caveat: this is an association, not proof. Richer countries both check more and record more, and IHME estimates for countries with little data lean on regional models. The middle quarters do not rise in a straight line.

-   For comparison, tested_blood_pressure: rho = +0.16, p = 0.053.
-   For comparison, tested_cancer: rho = +0.18, p = 0.037.
-   For comparison, tested_std: rho = -0.07, p = 0.452.
